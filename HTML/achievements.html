A.R. Rahman's Achievements

  • Two Academy Awards (Oscars)
  • Two Grammy Awards
  • BAFTA Award
  • Golden Globe Award
  • National Film Awards (India)
  • Padma Bhushan (India's third-highest civilian award)
  • Numerous Filmfare Awards
  • International recognition for "Slumdog Millionaire" soundtrack

Rahman is celebrated for his innovative music and global influence, making him one of the most respected composers in the world.

A.R. Rahman

A.R. Rahman is an Indian composer, singer, and music producer known for his unique blend of Indian classical music with electronic music and world music elements.

He is a recipient of numerous awards, including two Academy Awards, two Grammy Awards, a BAFTA Award, and a Golden Globe.

About A.R. Rahman

Born on January 6, 1967, in Chennai, India, A.R. Rahman started his career in the music industry at a young age. He gained international fame for his work on the soundtrack of the film "Roja" in 1992, which won him the National Film Award for Best Music Direction.

Rahman's music is characterized by its innovative use of technology and fusion of different musical styles. He has composed music for numerous films in various languages, including Tamil, Hindi, and English.
